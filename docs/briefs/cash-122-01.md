## Problem and decision (3–5 lines)

Research only, no build, no Toast or R365 change. It answers what the skill can read before anyone designs it. Steps:
1. **PC capture, Toast (00122 - Cary 1, both weeks):** sales summary by day with tenders (cash, card, other, External);
   Payments → Payout overview and the payout list with each payout's detail (which sales date it covers, fees, External);
   the drill-down behind "External (not settled by Toast)"; Cash & loss management → cash drawer overview / history per day,
   and any Toast deposit or "cash out / deposit" record and its fields. Exports (CSV/XLSX) where offered; record report names and paths.
2. **PC capture, R365 (CARY 1, same days):** Daily Sales Summary list, and one day's detail opened **view only** on its Deposits
   area: the fields a deposit entry needs, the Over/Short formula as shown, and the status/Complete control (not clicked).
   Any CARY 1 day up to 10/05 marked Completed: open one view-only and record who/when completed and what deposit shows.
3. **Optional, owner:** a bank statement CSV for the same two weeks, saved by the owner into the capture folder. If not given,
   "received at bank" stays `(?)`. The note never prints account numbers (last 4 only).
4. **Server, Researcher:** `docs/research/cash-122.md` with one row per day: Toast net sales, card, cash, External/other,
   Toast payout (date + amount) matched to its sales day, expected cash deposit (cash payments − payouts/paid-outs as Toast
   shows them), Toast-recorded deposit if any, R365 DSS deposit and Over/Short, bank amount if given. Rules stated with 3 real
   examples. Explain the External line from what Toast shows (`(?)` if not shown). Say who owns each setting seen (owner,
   franchise, R365, Toast). New facts go to `skills/fhs-skills/references/tech-stack.md`.
